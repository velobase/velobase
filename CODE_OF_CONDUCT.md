# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Velobase community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security contact. Maintainers will investigate promptly, respect the reporter's privacy, and take action proportionate to the circumstances, including warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from community spaces.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
